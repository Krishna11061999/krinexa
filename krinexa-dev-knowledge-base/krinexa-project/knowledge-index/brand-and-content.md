## Branding & design direction

Modern, premium, technology-focused — not a personal resume look.
Individual profile cards for team expertise. Technology icons used
sparingly and grouped logically. Project screenshots/video previews
for credibility. Clean, highly readable typography. Mobile-first.
Subtle animation only where it doesn't hurt performance.

## Content rules (hard constraints)

- Never publish fake client names, logos, testimonials, or results.
- Use measurable results only when they can be supported.
- Get permission before displaying client logos or confidential
  project screenshots.
- Keep team biographies consistent in tone; first-person only on
  individual profiles, "we" for organization copy.
- Keep technology lists updated as the team evolves.
- Office location kept at Sector 49, Noida level unless a precise
  address is explicitly approved for public display.
